Every currency figure in Tally. Space Grotesk, tabular figures, two decimals always.

```jsx
<Amount value={128.4} size="hero" />
<Amount value={-24} tone="owe" showSign />
<Amount value={12.5} tone="owed" size="lg" />
```

Color is the sign: mint means money coming to you, coral means money you owe, grey means settled. Don't also write the words "you owe" in coral next to a coral figure — pick one carrier.
