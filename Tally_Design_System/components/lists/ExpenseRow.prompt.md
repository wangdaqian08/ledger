The group feed row. Category disc on the left, your position on the right.

```jsx
<ExpenseRow title="Sichuan Rose" category="food" paidBy="Mei" date="Yesterday" yourShare={-18.4} />
```

Category colour comes from the person-hue ramp so the whole app stays in one palette. Never show the full expense total here — show *your* share; the total belongs on the detail screen.
